##Course Description  
The dynamics of romantic attraction and compatibility are complex and often confusing. But what do we know about what makes people drawn to potential partners and satisfied once they enter relationships? This special-problems seminar addresses this question by covering relevant psychological research about the concepts, predictors, and consequences of initial romantic attraction, relationship initiation, and long-term compatibility.
