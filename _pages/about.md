I am a postdoctoral research at University of Nebraska, Lincoln with the [UNL Family Development Lab] (https://familylab.unl.edu/) run by [Dr. Rebecca Brock] (https://psychology.unl.edu/person/rebecca-brock/). I received my PhD in Psychology from the University of Michigan in the [WHIRLab] (https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/whirl/) under [Dr. Amie Gordon] (https://lsa.umich.edu/psych/people/faculty/amie-gordon.html) and the [Personality, Relationships, and Hormones Lab] (https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/edelstein-lab/) under [Dr. Robin Edelstein] (https://lsa.umich.edu/psych/people/faculty/redelste.html). She studies individual differences in the context of romantic relationships, including how similarity influences compatibility.

This website includes my publications, CV, and contact information for research questions, collaborations, and speaking opportunities.
