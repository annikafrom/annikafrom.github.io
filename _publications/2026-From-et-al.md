Using an experimental design with 676 dating app users, this study examines how visual cues in dating profiles (i.e., hobbies and flags) influence perceived political ideology and romantic interest, and how these effects vary by race, gender, political ideology, and color-blind racial attitudes. Findings suggest that fishing photos and American flags are perceived as signals of conservatism, particularly among liberal daters, and that these perceptions may contribute to political homophily in romantic partner selection.
