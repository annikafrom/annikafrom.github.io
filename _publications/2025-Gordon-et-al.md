Given the importance of high-quality romantic relationships for health and well-being, researchers have spent decades examining factors that promote them. However, making more targeted predictions for particular groups of people or even specific couples—with a meaningful degree of accuracy—remains challenging. We argue that the field is now at a pivotal moment, as technological advances are transforming what is possible.
